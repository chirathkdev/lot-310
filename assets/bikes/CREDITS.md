# Bicycle

"Bicycle" by andyvargas (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bicycle-467c6873188044ee85066fa3cea957f5), as packaged for Gazebo by Allison Thackston
(https://fuel.gazebosim.org/1.0/athackst/models/bicycle), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: turned to face +Z, rescaled to 1.70 m long, texture resized to 1024 px and KTX2-compressed,
mesh compressed (meshopt); the page tints the frame per rider.
